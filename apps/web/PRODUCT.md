# Digital Eco — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Digital Eco is an Italian digital agency website. One-page marketing site showcasing services (web design, e-commerce, social media, advertising, SEO, branding, digital strategy), portfolio projects, and contact form. Goal: attract Italian SMB clients seeking digital transformation.

## Users
Italian small-to-medium business owners looking for a digital agency. Decision makers evaluating agencies by portfolio quality and perceived expertise. Desktop and mobile visitors, primarily from Google Ads and organic search.

## Brand
- **Tone**: Premium, confident, editorial. Modern Italian luxury meets digital craft.
- **Voice**: Professional but approachable. Italian language throughout.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS templates, cookie-cutter agency sites, overly playful/startup-y aesthetics.

## Visual Identity
- **Colors**: Dark green primary (#0A5C44), bright green accent (#2ECC71), light background (#F8F9FA), dark text (#1A1A2E)
- **Typography**: Instrument Serif (serif, display/headlines), DM Sans (body)
- **Feel**: Editorial, refined, modern luxury. White/light backgrounds with green accents.

## Register
brand

## Tech Stack
Next.js 15 (App Router, Turbopack), Framer Motion, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS utilities available.
